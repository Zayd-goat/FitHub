# FitHub 1.2.2

## Saved workouts
- Create a workout ahead of time and save it with a name such as Push Day or Leg Day.
- Saved workouts appear at the top of the Train tab.
- Start a saved workout directly when ready to train.
- Edit or delete saved workouts.
- Saved workouts are stored in Supabase and follow the signed-in account.

## Editing an active workout
- Change weight and reps while the workout is running.
- Add sets during the workout.
- Remove sets during the workout while keeping at least one set per strength exercise.
- Add exercises from a searchable exercise picker without stopping the workout timer.
- Remove an exercise from the active workout when more than one exercise remains.
- Cardio distance, duration, and load remain editable while training.
