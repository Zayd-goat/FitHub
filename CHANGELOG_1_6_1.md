# FitHub 1.6.1

- Supplement notifications now offer Taken and Reschedule actions; one-time later reminders do not change the normal daily schedule.
- Supplements support colour assignment and colour-coded calendar history.
- Step counter, step history, step groups, leaderboard navigation, sensor code and activity-recognition permission removed. Existing database rows remain retained.
- FatSecret proxy expanded for upgraded-account serving details, images and additional nutrients when the provider returns them.
- Food diary refreshed with clearer daily cards and water intake quick-add/undo.
- Challenges support 1–5 star creator/FitHub difficulty, official monthly lifecycle, age eligibility and completed challenge history.
- Workout owners can delete feed posts without deleting their underlying workout or PR history.
- Active workouts support Next, Last, Up and Down exercise reordering while preserving logged sets.
- Home no longer shows Clubs or Recent Workouts and focuses on training, progress and Journey reports.
- FTMS Bluetooth connection now requests Android Nearby Devices permission, checks Bluetooth state, validates the standard FTMS service and handles unsupported devices cleanly.
- Android versionCode 11; standalone release workflow retained.
