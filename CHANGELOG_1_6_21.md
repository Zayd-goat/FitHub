# FitHub 1.6.21 Changelog

## Home

- Implemented the approved Home composition without changing its agreed structure.
- Added the semi-flat navy/accent gym icon family for Journey, Nutrition, Supplements, Community Challenges, and Run Metrics.
- Enlarged and refined the week calendar, current-day pill, progress rings, weight-plate metric, stopwatch metric, typography, shadows, and spacing.
- Kept every card connected to its existing live destination and retained the dynamic week/workout data.

## Food

- Implemented the approved Food composition with its large diary illustration and four individual action cards.
- Added matching Search, Scan, Recent, Saved Meals, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, and Water artwork.
- Refined the connected meal timeline, larger add buttons, rounded cards, water glasses, progress track, and date strip.
- Preserved Food Search, barcode scanning, recent items, saved meals, adding/removing foods, water add/undo, and age protections.

## Navigation

- Updated the shared bottom navigation to the approved floating rounded dock with larger touch targets, active indicators, theme-aware vector icons, and raised Train action.
- The same dock is used across Home, Food, Friends, Train, You, and secondary pages.

## My Fitness Journey

- Added current-versus-previous weekly and 30-day comparisons.
- Added tap-selectable trend views for workouts, minutes, sets, and cardio distance.
- Added clearer report dates, private-history labelling, metric cards, comparison rows, and progress highlights.
- Adult accounts retain private lift and PR summaries. Younger accounts receive exercise/consistency highlights and technique-focused guidance without load-based records.

## Release

- Version `1.6.21`, Android version code `31`, iOS build number `31`.
- APK artifact: `FitHub-1.6.21-APK`.
- No new database migration, Edge Function, secret, or cron change is required for this UI release.
