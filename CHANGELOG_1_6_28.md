# FitHub 1.6.28 Changelog

## Friends feed

- Replaced the old underline tabs with a clear four-option segmented control.
- Added Feed filters for all posts, workouts, personal records and progress.
- Rebuilt workout posts as rounded cards with stronger hierarchy, custom stat icons and accessible actions.
- Preserved likes, comments, sharing, editing, deletion and count-visibility controls.

## Following and invitations

- Added a global friend search field and dedicated Following management cards.
- Kept separate post and PR notification preferences for every friend.
- Grouped gym invites and friend requests in one Invites view with visible counts.
- Added clear Accept, Decline, Confirm and Remove actions plus the existing gym-session planner.
- Preserved upcoming sessions, sent invitations, push responses and local reminders.

## My Posts

- Added a dedicated My Posts tab that never mixes in friends' posts.
- Added personal post, like and comment totals.
- Added own-post search, filters and grid/list layouts.
- Kept post editing, photo replacement, count privacy and deletion available from each own-post card.

## Visual system and release identity

- Added `src/components/FitHubSocialIcons.tsx` with original vector icons for the social experience.
- Restored the complete male Ab Wheel Rollout PNG inherited in truncated form from the previous package.
