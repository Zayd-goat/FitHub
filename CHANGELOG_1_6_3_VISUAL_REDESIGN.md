# FitHub 1.6.3 visual redesign

This release implements the approved FitHub concepts as working screens while preserving the existing app functions and Supabase data flows.

## Home

- Cleaner plan-first dashboard with anatomical workout hero.
- Compact weekly training calendar and direct Journey access.
- Quick-access cards for Journey, Nutrition and Supplements.
- Friends-feed shortcut and a separate link to full progress/PR reports.
- Existing workouts, reports, progress and streak data remain connected.

## Train

- Large two-column anatomical grid for Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs, Core, Full Body and Cardio.
- Selecting a muscle group reveals only matching exercises.
- Exercise list keeps equipment, target, add/configure and workout-builder behavior.
- Saved workouts, active workout recovery, exercise reordering, PR detection and FTMS cardio remain available.

## Food

- Weekly date strip and Today's Diary completion card.
- Search, Scan, Recent and Saved Meals quick actions.
- Expandable Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks cards.
- Existing logged-food removal is available from the meal cards.
- Visual water-glass tracker with quick add and undo.
- Expandable nutrition overview for adult accounts.
- Under-18 accounts continue to receive the safer meal-journal view without calorie or macro targets.
- FatSecret search, barcode scanning, custom foods, history, recipes and saved meals remain connected.

## Navigation

- Home, Friends, raised Train, Food and You remain the five destinations.
- Active state, permanent labels and pending friend-request badge remain enabled.

No new database migration is required when upgrading from the complete FitHub 1.6.2 package.
