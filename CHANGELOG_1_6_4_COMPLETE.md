# FitHub 1.6.4 complete update

## Visual redesign

- Home now follows the supplied dark gym concept: profile greeting, notification/settings icons, focused workout plan, weekly activity, custom Journey/Nutrition/Supplement icons, and real friend avatars.
- Train now uses the supplied two-column muscle grid, exercise-specific thumbnails, equipment filters, search, Saved Workouts access, and the existing workout builder/active-workout features.
- Food now follows the supplied diary layout with a week strip, diary progress, custom line icons, meal cards, proper glass-shaped water indicators, copy-yesterday, saved meals, searchable foods, barcode scanning, history, and an expandable overview.

## Social posts

- Post owners can delete workout posts.
- Comment authors can delete their own comments.
- Post owners can delete, hide, or unhide comments on their posts.
- Like and comment totals appear on workout posts.
- Post owners can hide either total from other users and can still see the hidden total themselves.

## Clubs

- Club membership is recalculated from completed workout history.
- Only the user's highest qualifying club for each supported major lift remains current.
- Reaching a new threshold replaces the previous current club and produces a level-up message after the PR message.
- Current club cards and popups show the live number of members at that exact level.
- Load-based Clubs remain unavailable to under-18 accounts.

## Supplements

- Notification actions retain Taken and Reschedule.
- Reschedule opens FitHub and offers one hour or two hours.
- The selected reminder is a one-time same-day notification; the normal daily time continues tomorrow.
- Marking Taken cancels that day's pending snooze.

## Preserved functionality

Authentication, profiles, saved workouts, workout editing/reordering, active workout recovery, PR graphs/sharing, cardio and FTMS Bluetooth, gym invites, Friends feed, challenges, themes, feature hiding, nutrition history, supplement/workout calendars, and immersive Android behavior remain present. Step counting and step leaderboards remain removed as requested.

## Safety and privacy

- Food diaries remain private by default.
- Calorie/macro targets and detailed numeric nutrition displays remain disabled for under-18 accounts.
- Provider secrets stay in Supabase Edge Function secrets and are not bundled in Android.
